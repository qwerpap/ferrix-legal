Privacy Policy

Ferrix: Stud & Metal Finder · updated September 29, 2026

Ferrix is made by Danil Chshavelev. This policy explains what the app does with data. Short version: your readings and walls stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • Magnetometer readings are processed live on the iPhone and are not uploaded.
  • Saved walls (name, date, the pass profile and marked lines), your settings and your two onboarding answers (what you are hanging, the wall type) are stored only on your device.
  • Ferrix does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. It has no accounts.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. To manage subscriptions and measure how the paywall performs, Ferrix uses Adapty as a processor. Adapty receives an anonymous app-generated profile ID, purchase and subscription status, device model, OS version, app version, country/region derived from the IP address, which onboarding screen was reached, and the two onboarding answers. Ferrix does not collect your name, email or advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Ferrix is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting data

Delete a wall with ✕ in Walls, or delete the app to remove everything on the device. To ask about data held by Adapty for your anonymous profile, write to netkaktakto@gmail.com and include the profile ID shown in the feedback email.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything changes. Questions: netkaktakto@gmail.com.
