Ferrix // data sheet

Ferrix reads your iPhone's magnetometer to find nails and screws. This sheet lists every piece of data the app touches and where it ends up.

DataWhere it livesLeaves the phone?
Magnetometer readings (µT)Processed live in memoryNo
Saved walls: name, date, pass profile, marked linesLocal database on the deviceOnly if you tap Share
Settings and the two onboarding answers (what you hang, wall type)On the deviceAnswers go to Adapty (below)
Purchase and subscription statusApple, plus AdaptyYes, to Adapty

Not used

Camera, microphone recording, location, contacts, photos, accounts. Click sounds are played through the speaker, nothing is recorded.

Adapty

Adapty (adapty.io) is our subscription processor. It receives a random app-generated profile ID, purchase status, device model, OS and app version, country or region inferred from the IP address, the onboarding screen reached and your two onboarding answers. No name, email or advertising identifier (IDFA); no tracking across other companies' apps or sites.

Sharing a wall

Share sends the marked pass through the standard iOS share sheet to the place you choose. We never see it.

Deleting

Remove a wall with the ✕ in Walls, or delete the app to clear everything stored on the phone. For Adapty data tied to your anonymous profile, email netkaktakto@gmail.com with the profile ID from the feedback email.

Children

Not directed to children under 13; no knowing collection of their data.
