Tips

  • Remove a magnetic case or MagSafe ring, then Set zero in the air, away from metal.
  • Hold the phone flat on the wall and slide slowly: about 2 seconds per foot (3 on plaster).
  • Ferrix finds the nails and screws that hold the wall board to the studs; where they repeat 16″ or 24″ apart, it marks a probable stud line.
  • It can't see wood, wires or pipes, or aluminium, copper, gold and silver.
