What the app does — and doesn't

Ferrix shows changes in the magnetic field measured by your iPhone's magnetometer. It detects iron and steel only (for example nails, screws, metal studs). It cannot see wood, wires, pipes, or non-ferrous metals. "Probable stud lines" are an estimate from fasteners repeating at standard spacing; they are not a guarantee. Always confirm before drilling, and switch off power and check for hidden cables and pipes with suitable tools. You use the readings at your own risk.
